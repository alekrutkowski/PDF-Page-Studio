How this app works

  1. Upload one or more PDFs with the button or by dropping files into the upload area.
  2. Each column is an output PDF. Drag pages inside a column to reorder them, or use each page's Move up and Move down buttons.
  3. Drag pages across columns to move pages across files. This is how you merge or redistribute pages.
  4. Use New empty PDF, or drop selected pages on the Drop pages here target, to split pages into a new output.
  5. Click pages to select them. Use Ctrl-click to toggle pages and Shift-click to select a range in the same column. Drag one selected page to move the whole selected group.
  6. Use a page card's Duplicate button to insert a copy immediately after that page. Use Copy to new PDF for selected pages when you want a separate output column.
  7. Use Go to page number at the top of a column to jump directly to an output page in that file.
  8. Use the Large thumbnails switch when you need sharper, easier-to-read page previews.
  9. Use the Theme switch to change between dark and light mode.
  10. Download one output PDF from its column, download a single merged PDF, or download all output PDFs as one ZIP.

Notes: the app runs in your browser and does not upload your PDFs to a server. Permission-protected PDFs are flattened to page images during export so their visible content is preserved; text on those pages will no longer be selectable.
